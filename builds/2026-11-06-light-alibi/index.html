LIGHT ALIBI

Proxy exits lie about where they are. Light in fibre doesn't.

You verify VPN and residential-proxy exits for a streaming platform. Each one claims a city. Databases can be faked; round-trip times can't be made faster than physics.

max distance = (RTT / 2) × 200 km/ms
  1. Probe the exit from landmark servers (tap them on the globe or in the list). Each ping draws a disk the exit must be inside.
  2. If the claimed city is outside any disk, the claim is physically impossible. Otherwise you can't disprove it (you can never prove it true).
  3. Pin where you think it really is, inside the region every disk allows, and file the report.

Drag to spin the globe, pinch or scroll to zoom. Probes are limited. Based on Constraint-Based Geolocation and Weinberg et al., "How to Catch when Proxies Lie" (IMC 2018).
