Red Ball Express · France, 1944

CRASH BOX

Your GMC "Jimmy" has a Warner T93 five-speed with no synchromesh. A gear only goes in when both halves of the dog clutch already spin at the same speed. Double-clutch, match the revs by eye and by ear, and keep the convoy rolling at 25 mph and 60 yards.

Sound on recommended: the engine is synthesised from its firing pulses. Keyboard: Shift is the clutch.

How to double-clutch

The tachometer has a white needle (engine) and an amber needle (gearbox input shaft - it parts from the engine when the clutch is down). Around the rim, ticks 1-5 show the rpm each gear needs at your road speed. A tick turns green when the input shaft is close enough to mesh.

  1. Clutch in, pull the lever to neutral.
  2. Clutch out (still in neutral): the input shaft now follows the engine.
  3. Upshift: let the revs fall to the next gear's tick. Downshift: blip the gas to raise them.
  4. Clutch in, push the lever into the gear. Clutch out, back on the gas.

Miss and the teeth clash: it grinds, the gearbox wears, and holding the lever there slowly "grinds it in". To pull away from a stop, hold the clutch and wait for the amber needle to stop before selecting 1st.

Expert: with the throttle feathered so the gears carry no load, the lever slips out without the clutch, and a gear goes in clutchless if the engine speed matches. Drivers called it floating the gears. You may need it.
